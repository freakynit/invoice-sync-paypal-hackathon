How it works

From folder to matched in three steps

A calm, repeatable flow — built for weekly bookkeeping and month-end alike.

1
Connect Drive + PayPal

Authorize read-only Drive access, tick your invoice folders or upload files directly, and link PayPal with login or app credentials.

2
Scan once, skip repeats

Files are fingerprinted by Drive ID + modified time. Only new or changed files are processed — unless you force a re-scan.

3
Review with confidence

Open the original beside the extracted fields, confirm the suggested payment or pick another. Your call sticks through future scans.

Matching, explained

A score you can audit, not a black box

Every candidate payment shows its signals and conflicts — strength, payer, amount, date, and the raw payment detail. Auto-confirm only happens when amount, currency, and identity all agree with no rivals.

Do I need to reorganize my Drive?

No. Select any folders — we'll include subfolders automatically and deduplicate overlaps. Add or remove folders anytime.

Can I upload invoices directly?

Yes — drop PDFs and images straight in, or sync a Drive folder. Both use the same extraction and review flow.

What counts as a match?

Exact amount and currency plus a strong identity signal (structured invoice reference or payer email), with no conflicting fields or rival candidates.

What happens on re-scans?

Unchanged files are skipped. Your manual confirms and unmatches are preserved; only new or changed items get re-scored.
